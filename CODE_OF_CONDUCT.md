# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Wiferry contributors are expected to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement should be
proportionate, documented privately, and focused on community safety.

For a confidential report, contact the maintainer through GitHub private
vulnerability reporting and state that the report concerns community conduct:
`https://github.com/SidUParis/wiferry/security/advisories/new`.

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
